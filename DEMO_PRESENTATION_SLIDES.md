### During Presentation

✅ **DO**:
- Make eye contact with audience
- Speak clearly and pace yourself
- Pause between concepts
- Emphasize key benefits
- Use real examples
- Show enthusiasm
- Encourage questions
- Be honest about capabilities

❌ **DON'T**:
- Rush through slides
- Use too much jargon
- Ignore questions
- Read slides verbatim
- Look at screen only
- Apologize for limitations
- Oversell features
- Assume understanding
